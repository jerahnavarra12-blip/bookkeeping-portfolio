The Process

What a realistic full-cycle month looks like.

Bookkeeping isn't a once-a-month task — it's a running cycle. Here's the actual rhythm behind a well-kept set of books.

Week 1

Open & Categorize

Pull in bank and credit card feeds, categorize new transactions against the chart of accounts, match receipts to charges, and clear the "for review" queue in QBO.

Week 2

A/P & A/R Maintenance

Enter and schedule vendor bills so nothing's paid late, send out customer invoices, and follow up on aging receivables before they go stale.

Week 3

Reconciliation

Reconcile every bank and credit card account against the statements, investigate discrepancies down to the transaction, and post adjusting entries with a documented trail.

Week 4

Close & Report

Review the trial balance, prepare the P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow reports, walk the client through the numbers, and lock the closed period.

This is the standard rhythm for a typical small-business client — the pace flexes with transaction volume and whatever reporting cadence we agree on.
